How it works

  1. Tap Record on your Watch. Talk normally. You can drop your wrist and keep going.
  2. Tap Stop. The note sends itself to your iPhone. No exporting, no syncing step.
  3. Read it, search it, play it. Each note gets a transcript and a short summary, all in one searchable list.

What's inside

  • One-tap Watch recording that keeps going with your wrist down, once you've started.
  • Automatic Watch → iPhone transfer. You'll see "Queued for iPhone" on the Watch when it's on its way.
  • Transcripts from Apple's speech recognition, on-device when your iPhone supports it.
  • Short summaries so you can see the gist before you open a note.
  • Search across every transcript and summary.
  • iPhone recording too. It keeps going if you lock the screen after you start.
  • Playback and sharing. Hear the original audio, or send the file anywhere through the share sheet.
  • No account. Your notes live on your iPhone.

Questions

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. You can record on iPhone too. The Watch is what makes it truly one tap, though.

Do I have to keep the Watch screen on?

Start the recording with the app open. After that, you can drop your wrist and keep talking.

Where do my recordings go?

To your iPhone, and that's where they stay. We don't run a server for your notes and don't upload them. Transcription uses Apple's speech recognition. When your iPhone can't do it on-device, Apple's service may handle it.

Is the summary written by AI?

It's a quick, automatic summary pulled from the start of your transcript, so you can tell notes apart at a glance. It isn't a chatbot rewriting your words.

What languages does it understand?

English (US) at launch.

Can I record meetings or other people?

aiNotetaker is built for your own voice notes. If you record other people, follow your local laws. Many places require everyone's permission.
