SiteProof

You did the work. Now you can show it.

SiteProof keeps every job's photos in one place, stamped with when and where they were taken, so proving the work takes seconds, not a search through your camera roll.

Sound familiar?

The 9 p.m. scroll.

The customer says the flashing was never replaced. You know you shot it. Now it's 9 p.m. and you're thumbing through 4,000 photos of your kids, receipts, and three other roofs.

The group-text gallery.

The office needs the "before" pictures for the adjuster. Two guys sent theirs by text, one emailed a blurry zip, and nobody's sure which house is which.

The report that eats your Saturday.

You promised the homeowner a write-up. That means copying photos into a document one at a time, typing dates by hand, and hoping it looks professional.

How it works

  1. Make a project for the job. Give it a name and a note. That's its home now.
  2. Add photos as you go. Each one keeps its capture time, plus location if you allow it. Tag it "before," "damage," or "final."
  3. Share it or print it. Send a view-only gallery link, or download a PDF report with one photo per page.

What's inside

  • One project per job. Photos never wander into the wrong folder.
  • Time and place on every shot. Capture time always. GPS when you turn it on.
  • Tags and order. Arrange photos the way you'd tell the story.
  • Guest gallery links. Customers, adjusters, or the office open it in any browser. They can look but can't change anything.
  • PDF reports. A cover page with the project summary, then a full page for each photo with its time, location, and tag.

Questions

When can I get it?

We're finishing the iPhone app now. Join the list and we'll send one email when it's in the App Store.

What does it cost?

Pricing isn't set yet. People on the waitlist will hear first.

Who is it for?

Small trade crews (roofing, restoration, general contracting, landscaping, solar) who want simple, trustworthy proof of work without a big system to learn.

Does it track my location?

Only when you're adding a photo, and only if you allow it. The location is saved with that photo so you can prove where it was taken. You can turn it off.

Who can see my photos?

You. A guest can see a project's gallery only if you send them the link. Anyone with that link can view the project's photos, including their GPS if it was captured, so share it with care.

Is there an Android version?

It's planned, but this first launch is iPhone.
