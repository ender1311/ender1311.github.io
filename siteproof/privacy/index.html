SiteProof Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026
Hosted at: https://dan-luk.com/siteproof/privacy

SiteProof ("SiteProof," "we," "us") is an app for documenting job sites with photos. It's run by Dan Luk, an individual developer. This policy explains what SiteProof collects, why, who it's shared with, how long it's kept, and how to have it deleted.

1. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name and email address, and your organization or team name, when you sign up or are invited.
  • Project details: project names and descriptions you type in.
  • Photos and videos you add to a project, plus the file name, file type, and size.
  • Tags and ordering you add to photos.

Information captured with your photos

  • Capture time: when the photo or video was taken or added.
  • Precise location (GPS): latitude and longitude, only if you allow location access and leave location tagging on. It's attached to that photo so the record shows where the work happened. SiteProof doesn't track your location in the background.

Information created as you use the service

  • Guest gallery links: a random link code tied to a project, plus when it was created and when it expires (if it does).

We don't collect contacts, health data, financial account numbers, or your camera roll beyond the photos you choose to add.

2. How we use it

  • To run the app: store your projects and photos, show them to you and your team, build PDF reports, and serve guest galleries.
  • To keep your account working and secure.
  • To answer your support requests.
  • To fix bugs and improve reliability (diagnostics, if enabled).

We don't sell your personal information. We don't use your photos or location for advertising. We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. Who can see your content

  • You, and people you invite into a SiteProof organization if you use one.
  • Anyone with a guest gallery link you create. Guest galleries are view-only and need no sign-in. Anyone who has the link can see the project name, description, photos and videos, capture times, tags, and GPS coordinates if they were captured. Share links only with people you trust.
  • PDF reports you download include capture times, GPS coordinates (when captured), and tags. Once you send a PDF to someone, it's out of our control.

4. Service providers

We use providers to host the service, store files, send email, and (if enabled) report crashes. They process data only to provide those services to us. Specific vendors aren't listed here until they're chosen.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety, or as part of a sale or transfer of the app (and we'd tell you).

5. How long we keep it

  • Projects, photos, and related details stay until you delete them or delete your account.
  • When you delete a photo, project, or account, we remove it from active systems and then from backups.
  • Guest links stop working once the project is deleted.
  • Support emails are kept as long as needed to help you, then deleted.

6. Your choices and deletion

  • Location: you can turn off location access for SiteProof in iOS Settings at any time. Photos added after that won't include GPS.
  • Delete content: you can remove single photos or whole projects in the app.
  • Delete your account: in the app, or email us from the address on your account, and we'll delete your account and its data.
  • Access or copy: email us and we'll help you export your data. You can also download PDF reports at any time.

Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU, or the UK), you may have extra rights to access, correct, delete, or move your data, or to object to how it's processed. Email us to use them. We won't treat you differently for doing so.

7. Children

SiteProof is a work tool for adults. It isn't directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age), and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, such as encryption in transit and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your sign-in details private and share guest links carefully.

9. International users

SiteProof is run from the United States. Your information may be processed in the U.S. or wherever our providers operate.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. For important changes, we'll also let you know in the app or by email.
