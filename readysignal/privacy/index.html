ReadySignal Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026
Hosted at: https://dan-luk.com/readysignal/privacy

ReadySignal is a morning readiness coaching app for iPhone, run by Dan Luk, an individual developer. This policy explains how ReadySignal handles your information, including health data from Apple Health. ReadySignal does not include an Apple Watch companion app; it reads Apple Health data your Watch (or other sources) already writes.

The short version: ReadySignal reads health data from Apple Health with your permission, and only reads it. It processes and stores that data on your iPhone. We don't upload your health data to our servers, sell it, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or use it for advertising.

1. What ReadySignal reads from Apple Health

Only what you allow in the iOS Health permission sheet:

  • Sleep: overnight sleep duration
  • Heart: heart rate variability (HRV) and resting heart rate
  • Activity: active energy
  • Workouts: your workouts, including duration and average heart rate

ReadySignal never writes data to Apple Health.

2. What ReadySignal stores

On your iPhone, in the app's private storage:

  • the health-derived daily values it needs for your 14-day baseline (for example, sleep minutes, HRV, resting heart rate, energy, and workouts) and the scores it calculates (Charge, Strain, Recovery Cue)
  • the event tags you choose (for example Travel, Alcohol, Late meal, Illness, Hard day, Late bed, Stressful day) and their dates
  • app settings: whether you finished onboarding, your data-source preference, and your plan status (Free or Pro)

We don't ask for your name, email, or location, and we don't require an account.

3. How it's used

Only to show you your Day Brief, activity band, sleep check-in, event-tag comparisons, and workout review. Calculations happen on your device.

4. Sharing

  • We don't share your health data with anyone. It doesn't leave your device through ReadySignal.
  • We don't use health data for advertising, marketing, or data mining (Apple's HealthKit rules forbid this, and we agree).
  • Purchases: if you buy an upgrade, Apple processes the payment. We get only Apple's confirmation of what you're entitled to, not your payment details.
  • Crash reports: if you've turned on "Share with App Developers" in iOS settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash and diagnostic reports. We don't add any other analytics or crash tools.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it. Because we don't hold your health data on our servers, there is usually nothing for us to disclose.

5. Backups and iCloud

ReadySignal doesn't sync your data to our cloud. Depending on your iPhone settings, iOS may include the app's on-device data in your device backup (iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup), which Apple controls.

6. How long it's kept

On your device until you delete it. The app compares against a rolling 14-day window.

7. Your choices and deletion

  • Stop access: turn off any data type, or all of them, under iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → ReadySignal (or Settings → Privacy & Security → Health). ReadySignal will show a repair notice instead of stale numbers.
  • Delete your data: delete the ReadySignal app. That removes its on-device storage. Your original data in Apple Health is untouched.
  • Requests: since we don't hold your data, we usually have nothing to delete on our end. You can still email us with any privacy question or request.

8. Not medical advice

ReadySignal gives coaching context about training load and recovery. It is not medical advice, a diagnosis, a treatment, or a medical device.

9. Children

ReadySignal isn't directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age), and we don't knowingly collect their information. Because data stays on the device, we don't receive it from anyone. If you have concerns, contact us.

10. Website and waitlist

If you join our launch waitlist at dan-luk.com, we collect the email address you enter, only to tell you when ReadySignal launches. You can unsubscribe at any time. Right now that waitlist is a mailto to the address below.

11. Changes

If we change how ReadySignal handles data (for example, adding optional cloud sync or a Watch app), we'll update this policy and the date above. We'll ask for your consent first wherever the law or Apple requires it.
