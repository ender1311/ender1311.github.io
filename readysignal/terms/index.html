1. What ReadySignal is

ReadySignal is an iPhone app. It reads data you allow from Apple Health and gives you a morning Day Brief: Charge, Strain, and a Recovery Cue (Push, Maintain, or Recover) compared with your own recent baseline. It also has an activity band, a sleep check-in, event tags, and workout review. There is no Apple Watch companion app in the first release. If you wear an Apple Watch, ReadySignal may read Health data that Watch already writes; the cue lives on your iPhone.
