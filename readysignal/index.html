How it works

  1. Connect Apple Health. ReadySignal tells you exactly what it reads (sleep, heart, activity, workouts) and why. It only reads. It never writes.
  2. Give it a few nights. Your Day Brief stays empty until there's enough data to compare against your own baseline. No made-up first-day scores.
  3. Read one line each morning. Charge, Strain, and a Recovery Cue: Push, Maintain, or Recover, with a "Low data" badge when it isn't sure yet.

What's inside

  • Day Brief: Charge, Strain, and one-sentence cue, measured against your last 14 days.
  • Today's activity band: a target Strain range from today's cue, with progress as your activity comes in.
  • Overnight check-in: last night's sleep vs your 14-day average, and how it nudged today's cue.
  • Event tags: Travel, Alcohol, Late meal, Illness, Hard day, Late bed, Stressful day. See how your next-day Charge compared. (That's a pattern, not a diagnosis.)
  • Workout review: your Watch workouts with heart-rate zone and Strain vs that day's cue.
  • Tells you when it's broken: if Health access drops, you get a clear repair step, not quietly stale numbers.

Questions

Do I need an Apple Watch?

ReadySignal reads from Apple Health on your iPhone. For sleep and heart-rate variability, that usually means wearing an Apple Watch. There is no ReadySignal Watch app in the first release.

Is there a Watch app?

Not in the first release. Your cue lives on your iPhone.

Where does my health data go?

It stays on your iPhone. ReadySignal reads it from Apple Health and works it out on the device. We don't upload it, sell it, or use it for ads.

Is this medical advice?

No. ReadySignal is coaching context for training and recovery. It isn't a diagnosis or a medical device. If something feels wrong, talk to a clinician.

What will it cost?

Today's Day Brief and activity band are planned to be free. Deeper history may be a paid upgrade later. Pricing isn't set.

Why 14 days?

It's long enough to know what normal looks like for you, and short enough to keep up with your training, travel, and life.
